Pre-Ordinals Onchain Bitcoin NFTs (2021-2022)

Before the first Ordinals inscription (14th December 2022) there was already a small batch of entirely onchain Bitcoin NFTs. Here they are.
IMPORTANT: many artworks & documents were uploaded onto Bitcoin between 2011 & 2018 (see Ciro's Bitcoin Inscription Museum). Early onchain works from 2013-2018 such as AtomSea & Embii, the OLGA Counterparty broadcast, Cryptograffiti etc - while secured by Bitcoin - were etchings rather than NFTs in the sense that they were not binded directly to a token/collectible.
